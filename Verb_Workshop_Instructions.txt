VERB WORKSHOP — CUMULATIVE VERB SPELLING

TO OPEN
1. Save Verb_Spelling_Workshop.html to your computer.
2. Double-click it to open in Firefox, Chrome, or Edge.
3. No internet connection, installation, or login is needed.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• Learn: five rule groups with 20 verbs each, including the exact original 20 irregular past-tense verbs.
• Guided practice: identify the rule and type the word; hints and retry are available.
• Mixed challenge: fill in past-tense or -ing blanks without a rule cue or hint.
• Progress: separate assisted practice, independent spelling checks, and rule identification.
• Targeted review: retry forms that have not yet had two clean independent checks.

RULE GROUPS
1. Add -ed/-ing without altering base letters, including x and two-consonant endings.
2. Silent final e: add -d for past; drop e before -ing.
3. Short-vowel, one-syllable verbs: double the final consonant before -ed/-ing.
4. Consonant + y: change y to i before -ed, keep y before -ing (OPTIONAL NEW RULE).
5. Irregular past tense: 20 specific verbs from the earlier Yesterday Lab.

NOTES FOR PARENTS
Start in Guided Practice with 10 mixed questions. Later try a 10-question Mixed Challenge.
The optional consonant + y rule is excluded from mixed sets by default until you're ready.
A form is marked '2 clean checks' after two first-try correct independent answers.
That is a practice milestone, not a validated long-term mastery score.
Progress is saved only in the current browser/device. Clearing browser site data can erase it.
This expanded app has its own saved progress; it does not import scores from the original Yesterday Lab.

WORD BANK (EXPANDED)
• 20 base verbs in each of five groups = 100 total unique base verbs.
• The 80 regular verbs have both past and -ing forms; the 20 irregular verbs have past forms only.
• 180 total testable forms.
• The optional consonant + y group is off by default in mixed practice.
• Previous progress for existing words is retained when replacing the HTML file in the same browser and location.
